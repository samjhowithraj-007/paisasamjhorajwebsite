Privacy policy

No account needed

Every lesson, calculator and the Money Planner is open to everyone. You never need to sign up to learn here.

What stays on your device

Lesson progress, and your Money Planner inputs if you tick "Remember", are saved only in your own browser. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

Email updates (only if you sign up)

If you enter your email to get new lessons, we use it only for those emails, after you confirm the link we send. Our email service, Brevo, stores it. We never sell or share it, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

When you buy

Payments are handled by Razorpay or Gumroad. They collect the details needed to take the payment and follow their own privacy policies. We receive your name, email, phone (if given) and what you bought, only to deliver your order, send receipts and help with refunds. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

No tracking

No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this site. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep basic technical logs to run its service.

Deleting your data

Write to samjhowithraj@gmail.com and we will delete your email and order contact details, except what the law asks us to keep for tax records.
