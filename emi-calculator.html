Common questions

How is home loan EMI calculated?

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of monthly payments. The calculator does this for you.

How can I reduce the total interest on a home loan?

A shorter tenure, a lower rate or part-prepayments all cut total interest. Even one extra EMI a year can shorten a long loan by several years.

Does the EMI stay the same if the rate changes?

For floating-rate loans, the bank may change the EMI or the tenure when the rate changes. Ask your bank which one it changes.
