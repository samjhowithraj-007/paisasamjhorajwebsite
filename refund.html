Refund and cancellation policy

Digital products (PDF book, sheets, courses)

If the file does not arrive, does not open, or is not what we described, write to us within 7 days of purchase. We will fix it or give you a full refund.

Because digital files cannot be returned, we do not refund when you simply change your mind after downloading.

Charged twice or by mistake

If you were charged twice for the same order, or charged by mistake, write to us within 7 days and we will refund the extra charge.

Cancelling an order

Digital orders are delivered right away, so they cannot be cancelled after payment. If a payment fails or is cancelled before it completes, no money is taken; if your bank still deducts it, it is usually reversed automatically within 5 to 7 working days.

Workshops or live sessions (if offered)

You can cancel up to 24 hours before the session for a full refund. If we cancel or move a session, you get a full refund or a seat in the new session, your choice.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are started within 2 working days and go back to the original payment method. Banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show it.

How to ask

Write to samjhowithraj@gmail.com with your name, the email or phone used for the order, and what went wrong. We reply within 2 working days.
