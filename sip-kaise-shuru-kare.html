SIP kaise shuru kare: start your first SIP in 6 steps

A SIP is the simplest way to start investing every month. Here is the order to do it in, in plain words. First make sure you have an emergency fund and health and term insurance; a SIP is for money you will not need for at least 5 years.

The 6 steps

  1. Finish KYC once. Keep your PAN, Aadhaar, bank account and mobile number ready. KYC is done online through any fund house or registered platform.
  2. Write down the goal. For example, child's education in 15 years. The goal decides how long the money stays invested.
  3. Choose the type of fund for that goal. For long goals, many beginners start with a broad index fund. For goals under 3 years, equity funds are usually not suitable.
  4. Pick the direct plan. It has a lower expense ratio than the regular plan of the same fund.
  5. Set the amount and date. Choose an amount you can keep paying in a bad month, and a date just after your salary arrives. Set up auto-debit (mandate) so you do not have to remember.
  6. Step up every year. Raise the SIP with your salary increase, even by 10%.

See your numbers

₹5,000 a month for 20 years at an assumed 12% a year could become about ₹46 lakh; with a 10% yearly step-up, about ₹93 lakh (illustrative only, returns are never guaranteed). Try your own numbers in the SIP calculator.

Common questions

SIP shuru karne ke liye kya chahiye?

PAN card, Aadhaar (for KYC), a bank account and a mobile number. KYC is done once and works for all mutual funds.

Direct plan ya regular plan?

A direct plan has no distributor commission, so its expense ratio is lower. Over 15 to 20 years this difference adds up. Our lesson on direct and regular plans explains it with numbers.

Kya SIP beech mein rok sakte hain?

Yes. You can pause or stop a SIP in most funds. The money already invested stays in the fund. Try not to stop when the market falls; that is when SIP buys more units.
