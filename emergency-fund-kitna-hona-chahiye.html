Emergency fund kitna hona chahiye?

An emergency fund pays for job loss, a hospital bill or an urgent repair without a credit card or a loan. It is insurance for your money plan: without it, one bad month forces you to sell investments at the wrong time.

How many months?

Your incomeMonths of essential expenses
Very stable job (government, PSU)about 4
Private-sector salaryabout 6
Self-employed or variable income9 or more

Add a month if there is only one earner or many dependants. Size it on essential monthly expenses, not on income.

Example: the Verma family

Essentials are ₹58,000 a month, with a private-sector salary. 6 months gives a target of ₹3.48 lakh. Saving ₹15,000 a month, it takes about 2 years.

Build it step by step

  1. First ₹50,000.
  2. Then one full month of essentials.
  3. Then the full target.

Common questions

Emergency fund kitna hona chahiye?

Around 4 months of essential expenses for a very stable job, about 6 months for a private salary, and 9 or more for self-employed or variable income. Add a month if there is one earner or many dependants.

Emergency fund kahan rakhein?

Somewhere safe and quick to withdraw: a savings account, a sweep-in FD or a liquid fund. Not in shares.

Should I invest before building it?

Build at least a first step (₹50,000, then one month of expenses) before investing. Without it, one bad month can force you to sell investments at the wrong time.
