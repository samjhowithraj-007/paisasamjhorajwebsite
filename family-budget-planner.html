Family budget planner for Indian families

The Money Planner puts your whole family's money on one page: income, monthly and yearly expenses, emergency fund, loans, insurance and goals. It shows your monthly balance and how long your savings would last. It runs only in your browser.

What it shows you

  • Where the salary goes each month, split into essentials, wants and savings.
  • Your emergency fund target and how far you are from it.
  • Loan EMIs as a share of income.
  • Whether your health and term cover look enough.
  • How much to save every month for each goal.

How to start

  1. Track one month of spending first.
  2. Enter income and essentials in the planner.
  3. Add loans, insurance and goals.
  4. Tick "Remember" if you want it saved in this browser.

Common questions

Is my data safe in the Money Planner?

Yes. Everything you type stays in your own browser. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and there is no sign-up.

What should a family budget include?

Income, essential expenses (rent or EMI, groceries, school fees, bills), wants, yearly costs such as insurance premiums, and a fixed amount for savings and goals.

Which budget rule should I use?

A simple start is to track one month, then split spending into essentials, wants and savings. The planner shows your own split instead of a fixed rule.
